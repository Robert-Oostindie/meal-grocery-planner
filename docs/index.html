How It Works

Three simple steps from meal planning to grocery shopping

1

Add Your Recipes

Create recipes with ingredients, quantities, and your preferred stores. Set up substitute groups for flexible options. Organize by categories that make sense to you.

  • Add ingredients with autocomplete from USDA database
  • Set quantities and units
  • Choose which store to buy each item
  • Create substitute groups (e.g., "Protein: Chicken or Tofu")
2

Plan Your Meals

Select which meals you want to make this week. Uncheck ingredients you already have. Add other items you need like paper towels or laundry soap.

  • Check meals you're making this week
  • Multiply recipes (make 2x or 3x portions)
  • Uncheck ingredients already in your pantry
  • Swap substitutes with one tap
  • Add non-food items to your list
3

Shop Your List

Your grocery list is automatically organized by store and aisle. Know exactly what to get at each store and where to find it.

  • Lists split by store (Aldi, Walmart, etc.)
  • Items organized by aisle (Produce, Dairy, Meat)
  • See quantities and units for each item
  • Quick links to Instacart/DoorDash for delivery
